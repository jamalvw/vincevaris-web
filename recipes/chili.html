chili

ingredients:

  • 1lb ground beef
  • 1 diced yellow onion
  • 10oz can of petite diced tomatoes with green chiles
  • 29oz can of tomato sauce
  • 15oz can of dark red kidney beans
  • 1 pack of chili seasoning (or look up how to make some)

steps:

  1. brown the beef on medium-high in a pot. i like to do everything in one pot, but it's up to you. do not break up the meat until the bottom has browned.
  2. saute the yellow onion with a little oil in a separate pan with a little salt and pepper, then add it to the pot
  3. drop the heat on the pot to low, then pour in the diced tomatoes, tomato sauce
  4. drain the can of beans, then add into the pot
  5. add the chili seasoning and stir until properly mixed in.
  6. let cook on low for about 30 minutes to 1 hour. enjoy. i recommend putting this with buttered rice it is insanely good
