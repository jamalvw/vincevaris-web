chili

ingredients:

  • 1lb ground beef
  • 1 diced yellow onion
  • 10oz can petite diced tomatoes with green chiles
  • 29oz can tomato sauce
  • 15oz can dark red kidney beans
  • 1 pack of chili seasoning (or look up how to make some)
  • 2 tbsp of Goya Adobo All-Purpose Seasoning
  • 1 packet of Goya Sazon with Coriander and Annatto Seasoning

steps:

  1. brown the beef on medium-high in a pot. i like to do everything in one pot, but it's up to you. do not break up the meat until the bottom has browned.
  2. once broken up, add a decent amount of the all-purpose seasoning (not TOO much) and stir it in
  3. saute the yellow onion with a little oil in a separate pan with a little salt and pepper, then add it to the pot
  4. drop the heat on the pot to low, then pour in the diced tomatoes, tomato sauce
  5. drain the can of beans, then add into the pot
  6. add the chili seasoning and sazon seasoning. stir until properly mixed in.
  7. let cook on low for about 30 minutes to 1 hour. enjoy. i recommend putting this with buttered rice it is insanely good
